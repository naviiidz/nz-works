Role Overview

I worked in an R&D environment focused on service-robot development, where the primary objective was to turn research ideas into practical, autonomous mobile systems capable of operating in real-world settings.

The role combined robotics engineering, software development, and iterative prototyping to improve robot behavior, perception, navigation, and human interaction.

Key Responsibilities

  • Developed autonomous navigation and motion-planning logic for service robots.
  • Improved localization, path planning, and system reliability through iterative testing.
  • Worked with perception and sensor-based inputs to enhance robot awareness in dynamic environments.
  • Integrated human-robot interaction and practical deployment behaviors for service applications.
  • Collaborated on design, prototyping, and field validation of mobile robotic systems.
